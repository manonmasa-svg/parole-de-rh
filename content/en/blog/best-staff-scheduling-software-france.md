> **In short:**
> 1. This comparison ranks 6 **staff scheduling software** tools on verifiable criteria checked on 16 November 2026: Combo, Skello, Planday, Kelio, Eurécia and Factorial.
> 2. Combo comes first: €68 per site per month for the Time plan, a plan announced with no commitment and alerts linked to the collective agreement.
> 3. Skello (scheduling from €79 per month, 14-day trial) and Planday (€2.99 per user per month, 30-day trial) follow. Kelio offers advanced scheduling from €5.75 excl. VAT per employee for 50 employees, with a 3-year commitment.
> 4. Eurécia and Factorial include scheduling in their HRIS. Factorial shows two different entry prices on its pricing page.

## Best staff scheduling software: how this ranking is built

Naming the **best staff scheduling software** means stating the criteria. Testimonials and ratings published by vendors are not used. The ranking uses four criteria that anyone can check on the vendor's website:

1. **Price transparency**: published price, billing unit (site, user, employee).
2. **Trial and commitment**: free trial, commitment period, possibility to cancel.
3. **Rule checks**: alerts on the collective agreement or working time.
4. **Published scheduling features**: automatic scheduling, staffing forecasts, publishing schedules to teams.

This rank measures the amount of verifiable information published, not the quality of the software in use. Recording hours actually worked is covered in a separate comparison, the [best time tracking software]({{< relref "best-time-tracking-software-france.md" >}}).

## Combo: a price per site and collective agreement alerts

Combo bills per site, which suits restaurant or retail networks. Two plans are published:

| Plan | Price checked on 16 November 2026 |
|---|---|
| Time | From €68 per site per month |
| People | From €89 per site per month |

- **Scheduling features announced**: collaborative, smart and predictive scheduling, alerts linked to the collective agreement, hour counters, schedule publishing, including by SMS. The detail by plan is in the vendor's comparison table.
- **Options**: time clock at €2 per employee per month, payslip distribution at €1.2, connector to the Silae payroll software at €1.5.
- **Included in all plans**: onboarding training, support, updates and hosting described as secure.
- **Terms**: the home page announces a plan "sans engagement, sans CB" (no commitment, no credit card).
- **Reported client base**: more than 13,000 companies and 150,000 employees.

On 16 November 2026, the website address of Snapshift, another scheduling tool, redirected to Combo's: Snapshift is therefore not covered separately.

## Skello and Planday: self-service scheduling plans

### Skello: from €79 per month

Skello publishes two scheduling-only plans, with 10% off for an annual commitment and a 14-day free trial:

- **Planning Standard**: from €79 per month, with week, month and day views, regulatory alerts predefined according to the collective agreement and a single schedule template.
- **Planning Max**: from €109 per month, with staffing forecasts, replacement suggestions and automatic schedule creation.

The Duo plans (scheduling and time clock) start at €99 per month. A tailored Enterprise plan targets organisations with 200 to 10,000 employees. The page does not say whether prices apply per site or per company. Nor does it say whether they exclude VAT, but Skello's terms and conditions (version of 1 December 2025, consulted on 23 November 2026) state that all prices exclude VAT.

### Planday: a price per user

Planday publishes its prices per user, with a 30-day free trial, no credit card and cancellation at any time:

| Plan | Price | Conditions | Points found |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | €2.99 per user per month | 5 users minimum | Scheduling, simple time tracking, compliance warnings |
| Plus | €4.99 per user per month + €25 monthly subscription | 10 users minimum | Advanced scheduling, leave and absences, integrations |
| Pro | Tailored | Large organisations | Automatic scheduling, custom roles |

The page does not say whether these prices exclude VAT. It also does not mention alerts linked to French collective agreements: the warnings concern the working time rules configured.

## Kelio, Eurécia and Factorial: scheduling in an HR suite

### Kelio: advanced scheduling for shift work

Kelio includes scheduling in its time management. According to its dedicated page, the tool creates schedules taking into account hours, skills, absences and legal constraints, can generate them automatically and compares planned hours with hours worked. The "time management and advanced scheduling" price scenario (posts, 3x8 shifts, modulation) is shown at €5.75 excl. VAT per employee per month for 50 employees, €2.60 for 200 and €1.55 for 1,000. The initial commitment period is 3 years, as detailed in the [Kelio review]({{< relref "kelio-review.md" >}}).

### Eurécia: the scheduling module of its HRIS

Eurécia offers a scheduling module in its HRIS. Its page announces real-time alerts when a legal rule is not respected, automatic synchronisation of absences, training, part-time work and public holidays, calculation of overtime and time off in lieu, and schedule access on mobile. The price of the module alone is not shown outside the simulator: the Business plan starts at €4.20 excl. VAT per employee per month, all modules combined. Details are in the [Eurécia review]({{< relref "eurecia-review.md" >}}).

### Factorial: unlimited schedules, a price to check

Factorial announces an unlimited number of schedules for fixed, flexible and variable hours, notifications to employees when a schedule is published or changed and filters by team and workplace. On 16 November 2026, the text of its pricing page showed "from €2.9 per month per user", while its title said "from €6.5". Other details of the offer are covered in the [Factorial review]({{< relref "factorial-review.md" >}}).

## What schedules must respect: maximum working hours

Scheduling software mainly helps avoid exceeding working time limits. According to the Service-Public guide on working time for a full-time private-sector employee, checked on 25 September 2025 and consulted on 16 November 2026:

| Rule | General limit |
|---|---|
| Maximum daily duration | 10 hours of actual work, unless exempted |
| Maximum in a single week | 48 hours |
| Average over 12 consecutive weeks | 44 hours per week |
| Exceptional exemption for one week | Up to 60 hours, with the labour inspectorate's approval |

Hours scheduled beyond the legal working week give rise to a pay increase, under the rules presented in the article on [overtime pay in France]({{< relref "overtime-pay-france-rules.md" >}}). The rules on [public holidays in France]({{< relref "public-holidays-france-employees.md" >}}) also matter when building schedules.

## Limits of this comparison

- No software was tested: the information is what vendors published.
- Prices cannot be compared directly: per site (Combo), per month with no unit stated (Skello), per user (Planday, Factorial) or per employee (Kelio).
- Skello and Planday do not say on their pricing page whether their prices exclude VAT. For Skello, the terms and conditions state that prices exclude VAT.
- Shiftbase, another scheduling tool, had no pricing page available in French on 16 November 2026 and was not included.
