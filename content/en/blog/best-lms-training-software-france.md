> 4. Lucca (€3.21 excl. VAT per employee) and Eurécia manage the training plan rather than e-learning. Didask shows "from €10,000 per year", while Rise Up, Docebo and Teach Up work on a quote basis.

## Best training software: how this ranking is built

Naming the **best training software** requires stating the criteria. Ratings from comparison sites, customer testimonials and performance statistics published by vendors are not used. The ranking relies on four criteria anyone can check on the vendor's website:

1. **Price transparency**: published price, billing unit (registered or active user, employee), user thresholds.
2. **Trial and commitment**: free trial, minimum contract length, monthly or annual billing.
3. **Scope**: delivering online training (LMS) or running the training plan.
4. **Declared security**: certification, data hosting location.

A vendor that publishes a full price grid ranks above one that refers to a quote. The rank measures the amount of verifiable information published, not the teaching quality of the software.

## LMS or training management software: two families of tools

The term "training software" covers two different needs, which should be separated before comparing prices.

An **LMS** (learning management system) is used to create, deliver and track online training: e-learning modules, learning paths, quizzes, virtual classrooms, certificates. 360Learning, iSpring LMS, TalentLMS, Moodle, Didask, Rise Up, Docebo and Teach Up belong to this family.

**Training management software** is used to run the company's training plan: catalogue, employee requests, manager approval, session organisation and budget tracking. The Training modules of Lucca and Eurécia belong to this second family.

A company may need both. Lucca, for example, says its catalogue can contain internal, external, online or API-synchronised training.

| Need | Type of tool | Software in this comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Create and deliver e-learning modules | LMS | 360Learning, iSpring LMS, TalentLMS, Moodle, Didask, Rise Up, Docebo, Teach Up |
| Train external customers or partners | LMS with external audiences | 360Learning, Docebo (Extended Enterprise add-on), Rise Up (monetisation) |
| Collect requests and build the training plan | Training management | Lucca, Eurécia |
| Track the training budget and funding | Training management | Lucca (own funds, OPCO, grants), Eurécia |

## 360Learning: a published price and no annual commitment

360Learning publishes three plans. The **Team** plan, presented as suited to SMEs and training organisations, costs €8 per user per month, up to 100 users. The Business and Enterprise plans are available on demo request.

According to the FAQ on the pricing page, consulted on 24 November 2026:

- **Commitment**: the Team plan is billed monthly, with no annual commitment, and can be cancelled at any time. Business and Enterprise plans generally use annual contracts.
- **Trial**: 30-day free trial, with no set-up fees.
- **Users**: no minimum. The contract can count registered users, monthly active users or a combination of both.
- **Security**: the vendor states that it complies with GDPR and with the SOC 2 and ISO 27001 standards.

The platform includes an authoring tool with AI-assisted creation, a SCORM player, and management of in-person and virtual classes. It integrates with Workday, SAP SuccessFactors and BambooHR on the HR side.

## iSpring LMS, TalentLMS and Moodle: public price grids or open code

### iSpring LMS: a sliding price per active user

iSpring publishes a grid for its Business plan, billed annually:

| Number of users | Price per user per month | Price per year |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | €6.61 | €7,936 |
| 300 | €4.25 | €15,312 |
| 500 | €3.78 | €22,665 |

Only active users are billed: a user counts if they logged in at least once in the month. Beyond the planned number, new users can no longer log in. The Enterprise plan is on quote. The vendor offers a 30-day free trial and announces a money-back guarantee. The page does not say whether prices exclude VAT.

### TalentLMS: a free plan for small teams

TalentLMS shows its prices in US dollars. On 24 November 2026, billed annually:

- **Free**: up to 5 users and 10 courses, no credit card.
- **Core**: $119 per month for 1 to 40 users ($1,428 per year).
- **Grow**: $229 per month for 1 to 70 users ($2,748 per year).
- **Pro**: $449 per month for 1 to 100 users ($5,388 per year).
- **Enterprise**: from 1,000 users, on request.

Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. All plans include unlimited storage and SCORM 1.2, Tin Can and cmi5 support.

### Moodle: open source software to host

Moodle is downloaded and installed on your own server, which requires a web server with PHP and a database. The latest stable version shown on 24 November 2026 was Moodle LMS 5.3.1+.

To avoid installation, the hosted MoodleCloud service offers a 28-day trial with no credit card. Its prices could not be checked, as the page is protected by an anti-bot check. The real cost of Moodle therefore depends on hosting and maintenance, in-house or outsourced.

## Lucca and Eurécia: running the training plan in the HRIS

### Lucca: the Training module at €3.21 excl. VAT

Lucca's Training module, "catalogue and request tracking", costs €3.21 excl. VAT per employee per month according to the indicative prices for companies with more than 100 employees. The price matches the one checked on 25 October 2026 in the [Lucca review]({{< relref "lucca-review.md" >}}).

The module lets companies build a training catalogue, collect employee requests, have managers prioritise them and schedule sessions over the year. Budget tracking separates forecast, committed and actual spending, as well as funding sources (own funds, OPCO, grants).

The module works with Lucca Performance: training wishes expressed in reviews feed the plan, and the training history is used to prepare mandatory reviews.

### Eurécia: a module of the HR platform

Eurécia offers a training management module in its HRIS: building the training plan, calculating costs, tracking the budget, catalogue and collecting certifications. Its Business plan, for 11 to 500 employees, is shown from €4.20 excl. VAT per employee per month for all modules, with a sliding scale. The 30-day free trial is presented as commitment-free.

## Didask, Rise Up, Docebo and Teach Up: quote-based offers

- **Didask**: three offers (Training, Coaching, Training + Coaching). The page shows a price "from €10,000 per year", depending on the number of users and use cases. The vendor announces no set-up fees. A promotional offer (Coaching included with any Training subscription before 31 December 2026) appears at the top of the page.
- **Rise Up**: custom pricing plan, two service levels (Standard and Prime). The vendor announces ISO 27001 certification, GDPR compliance and hosting in Europe.
- **Docebo**: Elevate and Enterprise plans on quote. Users are counted if they are active in the month or in the year, depending on the contract. The minimum contract length is one year, and the FAQ states that most customers sign for 3 to 5 years.
- **Teach Up**: two offers, Adaptive Training Studio (authoring tool compatible with other LMSs) and Adaptive Training Platform (full LCMS), both on price request.

## What French law says about employee training

Training software is used first to implement the **skills development plan** (plan de développement des compétences), which replaced the training plan in France. According to the Service-Public page checked on 13 March 2026, it includes three kinds of actions:

| Type of training | Main rule |
|---|---|
| Mandatory training | Required to perform an activity under a legal text. Pay is maintained. The employee's refusal is misconduct that may justify dismissal |
| Non-mandatory training | Optional action at the employer's initiative. Refusal is neither misconduct nor grounds for dismissal. Pay is maintained if it takes place during working hours |
| Skills assessment and VAE (accreditation of prior experience) | Possible within the plan, with the employee's agreement |

In all cases, training costs under the plan are paid by the employer.

The question often arises with e-learning, which employees can follow from home. Only non-mandatory training can take place outside working hours, with the employee's written agreement. Without a collective agreement, the limit is 30 hours per year per employee, or 2% of the annual allowance for employees on a fixed-days contract.

Training needs are also discussed during the [career path review]({{< relref "career-path-review-france.md" >}}), which the plan may take into account. Employees also have their own [CPF personal training account]({{< relref "cpf-personal-training-account-france.md" >}}) for training at their own initiative.

## Which training software for which situation?

| Situation | Software to consider | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Small team, limited budget | TalentLMS, Moodle | Free plan up to 5 users, or open source software |
| SME wanting a price known in advance | 360Learning, iSpring LMS | Published prices per user, 30-day trials |
| Company already using an HRIS | Lucca, Eurécia | Training module linked to employee records and reviews |
| Large organisation, external audiences | Docebo, Rise Up, 360Learning (Enterprise) | Custom offers, integrations, dedicated support |
| Training built around AI and learning design | Didask, Teach Up | Authoring tools and personalised paths announced |

For a company looking for a broader HR tool, the comparison of the [best HRIS software in France]({{< relref "best-hris-software-france.md" >}}) covers platforms that combine employee records, absences, reviews and, depending on the vendor, training.

## Limits of this comparison

- Prices cannot be compared directly: per registered or active user (360Learning, iSpring LMS), per user tier (TalentLMS), per company employee (Lucca, Eurécia) or on quote.
- The prices of 360Learning, iSpring LMS and TalentLMS do not say whether they exclude VAT. TalentLMS prices are in dollars.
- MoodleCloud prices could not be consulted.
- No software was tested. The features and certifications mentioned are those announced by the vendors.
