> **In short:**
> 1. There is no universal **best recruitment software**. This comparison ranks six ATS on verifiable criteria, taken from the vendors' French websites on 21 October 2026.
> 2. We Recruit comes first: it is the only specialist tool in the comparison to publish its prices (from €2,070 excluding tax per year), with a 30-day free trial.
> 3. Flatchr follows, with a 14-day trial, a no-commitment plan and more than 160 job sites connected, according to the vendor.
> 4. Four vendors out of six work on a quote basis: comparing them means requesting offers on a like-for-like basis.

## Best recruitment software: how this ranking is built

Naming the **best recruitment software** means saying on which criteria. User reviews and ratings displayed by vendors are not used, because their origin cannot be checked. Four verifiable criteria were used.

1. **The published price**: does the vendor display a price without requiring a quote?
2. **Trial and commitment**: can the tool be tested, and for how long must one commit?
3. **Announced features**: job posting, candidate database, collaboration, artificial intelligence.
4. **The companies targeted**: size, sector, hiring volume.

| Rank | Software | Main reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | We Recruit | Only specialist ATS in the comparison to publish its prices, 30-day free trial |
| 2 | Flatchr | 14-day trial, no-commitment plan, set-up and training costs announced as included |
| 3 | Lucca | Public price and no minimum term, but a module of an HR suite rather than a standalone ATS |
| 4 | Teamtailor | Unlimited users and job offers, AI included, security certifications displayed, price on quote |
| 5 | Taleez | Unlimited users and hiring, human support, price on quote |
| 6 | Recruitee | Plans described in detail and free trial, but no published price |

This rank measures the amount of verifiable information published, not the quality of the tool in use. A company hiring internationally will look at Teamtailor, whatever its rank here.

## We Recruit: published prices and a 30-day trial

We Recruit presents itself as recruitment software designed to structure and speed up hiring. It is the only specialist ATS in the comparison to display its prices.

| Plan | Price found | What is announced |
|---|---|---|
| Essentiel | From €2,070 excl. tax per year | Unlimited users, 2 recruiter calendars, multi-channel posting, collaborative recruitment between HR and managers, set-up included |
| Avancé (built-in AI) | From €3,270 excl. tax per year | Essentiel content, 5 recruiter calendars, application analysis and scoring |
| Premium - Scale | Not found | Multi-entity or high-volume organisations |

- **Commitment**: annual for the priced plans.
- **Trial**: free for 30 days, with no commitment.
- **Target**: the Essentiel plan is presented as ideal for SMEs. One offer is also aimed at recruitment firms and temporary work agencies.

## Flatchr: no commitment, for frontline jobs

Flatchr targets SMEs and mid-sized companies in operational sectors. The vendor highlights its fit with franchise networks, where each outlet recruits locally.

- **Price**: on quote.
- **Trial and commitment**: 14-day free trial, then a no-commitment plan that can be cancelled at any time, or a premium plan with an annual commitment.
- **Included according to the vendor**: deployment, configuration, training, maintenance, updates and return of data.
- **Posting**: more than 160 job sites connected, including LinkedIn, Indeed and Google for Jobs.
- **Features**: referrals, sourcing, CV search, text messages, multiple accounts, mobile app, AI-generated job offer templates.

The vendor reports more than 3,000 client companies.

## Lucca: a recruitment module within an HR suite

Lucca is not a standalone ATS: its Recruitment module, which manages candidates and open positions, is part of a broader HR suite, covered in the comparison of the [best HR software]({{< relref "best-hr-software-france.md" >}}).

- **Price**: €3.21 excl. tax per employee per month, an indicative rate for a company with more than 100 employees.
- **Commitment**: no minimum term.
- **Benefit**: for a company already using Lucca for leave or employee records, recruitment is added in the same environment. The vendor also announces more than 300 integrations.

## Teamtailor: a very complete tool, designed in Sweden

Teamtailor presents itself as all-in-one recruitment software for companies of all sizes.

- **Price**: on quote.
- **Included**: unlimited job offers and users, support for all users, more than 250 features, unlimited use of an AI assistant, automated workflows. Approval circuits are part of the Premium plan.
- **Security**: the vendor displays ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 certifications and SOC 2 compliance. Its operations are based in Sweden.
- **Reported clients**: more than 13,000 companies worldwide.

## Taleez: unlimited users and human support

Taleez, founded in 2016 according to the vendor, offers a common base supplemented by modules, rather than a series of plans.

- **Price**: on quote, with adapted conditions for some organisations such as associations.
- **Included**: unlimited hiring and users, dedicated human support, multi-posting of job offers, candidate database, careers site.
- **AI**: note-taking during interviews, suggestion of candidates close to the offer in the database, help writing job offers.
- **Reported clients**: more than 700 companies and more than 20,000 active users.

## Recruitee: detailed plans, no displayed price

Tellent Recruitee describes the content of its plans precisely, but displays no price on the page consulted.

- **Start plan**: one-page careers site, posting on several job sites, collaboration tools, unlimited storage, live chat and local support.
- **Advance plan**: unlimited job offers, multilingual careers site, AI translation of job offers, knockout questions, advanced reports.
- **Trial**: a free trial is offered, in addition to the demo.

## Which recruitment software for which situation?

- **An SME that wants to know the cost before any sales contact**: We Recruit is the only ATS in the comparison to publish its prices.
- **A network of outlets that hires a lot of frontline staff**: Flatchr targets this profile, with a no-commitment plan.
- **A company already equipped with Lucca**: the Recruitment module is added to the existing suite.
- **A company hiring in several countries**: Recruitee highlights a multilingual careers site and AI translation of job offers. Teamtailor reports more than 13,000 client companies worldwide.
- **An association or a small organisation**: Taleez mentions adapted conditions for some organisations such as associations, to be confirmed with the vendor.

## Recruitment software: criteria for choosing well

### 1. The volume and type of hiring

A company hiring a few managers a year does not have the same needs as a restaurant network hiring every week. Volume points towards a monthly no-commitment plan or an annual subscription.

### 2. Posting and compliance of job offers

An ATS posts offers on several sites at once. Each advert remains subject to French [job posting rules]({{< relref "job-posting-rules-france.md" >}}): it is dated, written in French, describes the job and contains no discriminatory criteria.

### 3. Candidate data

The software stores CVs, interview notes and contact details. It should make it possible to collect only useful information, in line with the rules on the [information an employer can ask a job candidate]({{< relref "information-employer-can-ask-job-candidate.md" >}}), and to delete applications in compliance with the GDPR, as covered in the article on [HR software and personal data]({{< relref "hr-software-personal-data-gdpr.md" >}}).

### 4. Artificial intelligence features

Several vendors in the comparison announce automatic sorting, scoring or matching of applications. The EU AI Act classifies [AI used in recruitment]({{< relref "ai-recruitment-eu-ai-act.md" >}}) among high-risk systems, with obligations applying from 2 December 2027 according to the European Commission's timetable. It is worth asking the vendor how it is preparing.

### 5. The real cost

On a like-for-like basis, compare the subscription, the number of users included, set-up fees and the cost of add-on modules (text messages, sourcing, careers site).

## Limits of this comparison

- No software was tested: the comparison describes what vendors publish.
- Client numbers and certifications are reported by the vendors.
- The prices found may change and do not cover the same scope: they should be confirmed by a quote.
- Other recruitment software exists and is not mentioned here.
