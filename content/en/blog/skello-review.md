> **In brief:**
> 1. **Skello** is a French staff scheduling, time clock and payroll preparation software for frontline teams. The vendor claims 30,000 teams and 700,000 employees as users (checked on 23 November 2026).
> 2. Published plans start at €59 per month (Badgeuse Standard, time clock) and €79 per month (Planning Standard), with a 14-day trial and a 10% discount for an annual commitment. The price unit is not stated.
> 3. Scheduling alerts follow the collective agreement. The software calculates payroll variables and exports them to Silae, Sage, ADP, Cegid Quadra, PayFit and other payroll tools.
> 4. Points to watch: the trial becomes a subscription unless the customer acts, the termination notice reaches 3 months on an annual commitment and data is hosted by Amazon Web Services in Ireland, with no security certification mentioned.

This **Skello review** relies only on the vendor's public pages, consulted on 23 November 2026: home page, pricing, payroll preparation, integrations, DPAE (French pre-hiring declaration), AI assistant, security FAQ, terms and conditions and legal notice. The software was not tested and Parole de RH has no commercial relationship with Skello. Customer testimonials and the average rating shown on the vendor's website are not included.

## Skello plans and pricing

The pricing page presents three families of plans, each in a Standard and a Max version, plus a custom Enterprise plan for 200 to 10,000 employees.

| Plan | Main content | Price checked on 23 November 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Planning Standard | Compliant schedule, time and absences, payroll variables, mobile app | From €79 per month |
| Planning Max | Planning Standard, staffing forecasts, automatic scheduling, e-signature of timesheets, unlimited AI assistant | From €109 per month |
| Badgeuse Standard | Time clock on tablet and mobile, geolocation when clocking in, hours tracking | From €59 per month |
| Badgeuse Max | Badgeuse Standard, time and absences, payroll variables, report for the accountant, unlimited AI assistant | From €89 per month |
| Duo Standard | Scheduling and time clock, time and absences, payroll variables | From €99 per month |
| Duo Max | Duo Standard, forecasts, automatic scheduling, e-signature of timesheets, unlimited AI assistant | From €129 per month |
| Enterprise | Custom plan, connection to HR tools, dedicated expert | On quote |

Three options can be added: Recruitment from €40 per month, Payroll service from €20 per month per employee, and RH Expert from €20 per month. A 10% discount applies with an annual commitment.

The pricing page does not say whether these prices apply per site, per team or to the whole account. The terms and conditions state that all prices exclude VAT and that the contractual price is set in the order form. The detailed feature table on the same page uses different plan names (Basic, Success, Premium), which makes it harder to read.

## Trial, commitment and termination

Skello offers a 14-day free trial on the plan of the customer's choice. According to the FAQ on the pricing page, at the end of these 14 days the trial automatically turns into a subscription if the customer takes no action.

The terms and conditions of service, in their version of 1 December 2025, set the following rules:

| Contract point | Rule found |
|---|---|
| Initial term | Set in the order form |
| Renewal | Automatic, for periods of one month (monthly commitment) or 12 months (annual or multi-year commitment) |
| Termination notice | 1 month before the end date for a monthly subscription, 3 months for an annual or multi-year subscription |
| Missed notice | Termination takes effect at the end of the following period |
| Form | Registered letter, termination form provided on request or email to the address given in the terms |
| Price changes | Increase possible once per calendar year, and at any time if Skello's external costs change |
| Payment | SEPA direct debit |

For seasonal businesses, the subscription can be paused: the account is suspended and the next invoice is postponed by the same length of time. The customer must have been a client for at least 3 months, plan no contract change, and the pause cannot exceed 9 months over a rolling 12 months.

## Scheduling, time clock and time management

Scheduling is at the heart of Skello. Schedules are built by week, month or day, down to the quarter hour, with week duplication, templates and management of employee availability.

Scheduling alerts are predefined according to the company's collective agreement and can be customised. A lock system lets managers flag that a day is ready, prevent changes to a week or block a period already processed in payroll. These checks cover in particular **overtime**, daily span and working time.

The time clock works on tablet and mobile, with geolocation when clocking in. Actual hours then feed time tracking, paid leave and RTT (reduced working time) counters, monthly or annual working time arrangements and the public holiday counter.

In the Max plans, Skello adds staffing forecasts, automatic schedule creation and e-signature of timesheets. The final schedule is sent to employees by email, text message or mobile app, with a limit of 200 text messages per month in the entry-level plan of the detailed table.

## Payroll preparation and integrations

Skello automatically calculates variable pay items (overtime, absences, bonuses, meals) according to the collective agreement, then groups them in a customisable payroll report, by organisation, site or employee.

This report is exported in the format of the company's payroll software. The integrations page lists ADP, Cegid Quadra, Nibelis, PayFit, Sage, SD Worx, Silae, Talentia and Yeap among the connected payroll tools. Payslips can then be distributed to employees from Skello.

The software does not calculate payslips itself. To have them produced, the Payroll service option, from €20 per month per employee, relies on a payroll manager from a partner accounting firm chosen by the company.

The RH Expert option adds document generation from templates, automatic distribution of contracts and payslips, and automatic sending of the DPAE (pre-hiring declaration) to Urssaf from the employee record, with tracking of the acknowledgement of receipt.

## Skello's AI assistant

The AI assistant answers questions in natural language about employees and schedules, based on the account's data and the user's access rights. It also flags items that may affect payroll, upcoming contract deadlines and workload imbalances.

The vendor claims a response within 5 seconds at most and more than 90% of correct answers on the first try. These figures are declared by Skello, with no published measurement method. The assistant is unlimited in the Max plans.

## Onboarding and support

According to the FAQ on the pricing page, each new customer gets guided onboarding, a dedicated contact person and training resources (knowledge bases, webinars). Support is available by chat, email and phone, Monday to Friday from 9:30 am to 6 pm. The Enterprise plan adds priority support with a dedicated expert.

## Data security and hosting

Skello's security FAQ provides the following information:

- **Hosting**: Amazon Web Services, with a data centre located in Ireland.
- **Transfers outside the European Union**: US subcontractors are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
- **Protection**: encrypted data, limited and logged staff access, double firewall, regular penetration tests carried out by different companies.
- **Retention**: for the duration of the contract, then return or destruction of the data according to the customer's instructions.

No security certification (ISO 27001 or other) is mentioned on that page. The employer's obligations regarding this data are detailed in the article on [HR software and GDPR]({{< relref "hr-software-personal-data-gdpr.md" >}}).
