## What is HRIS software?

An **HRIS**, or human resources information system (SIRH in French), brings employee data and everyday HR tasks together in a single tool. It replaces scattered spreadsheets and email exchanges for leave requests or administrative documents.

The term covers tools of very different sizes. Some are limited to employee records and absences, others cover the whole employee life cycle, from recruitment to training. The article on [HR management software]({{< relref "hr-management-software-france.md" >}}) details these functions one by one.

| Module | What it handles |
|---|---|
| Core or personnel records | Administrative data, documents, org chart, directory |
| Leave and absences | Online requests, balances, approval circuits |
| Time and activities | Clocking in, timesheets, scheduling |
| Talent | Reviews, objectives, skills, training |
| Recruitment | Job offers, applications, candidate tracking |
| Payroll | Payslips and social declarations, only in some HRIS |

## Best HRIS software: how this ranking is built

Naming the **best HRIS software** means saying on which criteria. User reviews and ratings displayed by vendors are not used, because their origin cannot be checked. Four verifiable criteria were used.

1. **The published price**: does the vendor display a price without requiring a quote?
2. **Trial and commitment**: can the tool be tested, and for how long must one commit?
3. **Displayed hosting and security**: hosting location, certifications.
4. **Announced modules**: from employee records to payroll.

| Rank | Software | Main reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lucca | Only vendor to publish a price for each module, with no minimum term |
| 2 | Eurécia | Public entry price, 30-day trial, no commitment, hosting announced in France |
| 3 | Factorial | Public entry price and free trial |
| 4 | Sesame HR | Detailed plans, free trial and ISO 27001 certification, but no published price |
| 5 | Cegid HR | Hosting in Europe and ISO 27001, but price on request and aimed at large organisations |

This rank measures the amount of verifiable information published, not the quality of the tool in use. A mid-sized company that wants payroll and HR in the same tool will look at Nibelis first, whatever its rank here.

## HRIS: criteria for choosing well

### 1. The modules actually needed

An HRIS is chosen first on the processes to equip. Paying for an unused performance module increases the bill without any benefit.

### 2. Payroll: built in or connected

An HRIS without payroll must pass absences and variable pay items to the payroll software. The quality of this link avoids re-entering data and errors on payslips.

### 3. Personal data

An HRIS holds sensitive data: address, social security number, absences, reviews. The rules to follow are covered in the article on [HR software and personal data]({{< relref "hr-software-personal-data-gdpr.md" >}}). Hosting location and security certifications are questions to ask each vendor.

### 4. Recruitment

Several HRIS include a recruitment module. For a high volume of hiring, a specialist tool may be more suitable, as shown in the comparison of the [best recruitment software]({{< relref "best-recruitment-software-france.md" >}}).

### 5. Total cost

The price per employee does not tell the whole story: set-up fees, support, add-on modules and the number of billed users all change the final budget.

## Limits of this comparison

- No software was tested: the comparison describes what vendors publish.
- Client numbers, certifications and hosting are reported by the vendors.
- The prices found do not cover the same scope and may change: they should be confirmed by a quote.
- Other HRIS exist. Personio could not be reviewed, as its French page was not accessible on 21 October 2026.
