> **In short:**
> 1. **HR management software** centralises employee data and automates the tasks of the personnel department: file, leave, time, reviews, training, payroll preparation.
> 2. It is not mandatory, but it helps meet precise obligations: single staff register from the first employee, monthly DSN, career path review every 4 years.
> 3. No independent statistic identifies the most used software. Vendors state between 4,500 and 22,000 client companies depending on the case, figures taken on 7 October 2026.
> 4. Published entry prices range from 3.05 to 6.90 euros per employee per month, for different scopes.

## What is HR management software?

**HR management software** is a computer tool that brings together information about employees and automates the administrative tasks of the human resources department. It is also known by the acronym HRIS, for human resources information system (SIRH in French).

The CNIL, the French data protection authority, points out that these tools contain a great deal of information about employees. The employer can record in them the data needed for the administrative management of staff, the organisation of work and social benefits.

### HR management software, HRIS, payroll software: the differences

| Term | What it covers |
|---|---|
| HR management software | General term for any tool that manages HR data and processes |
| HRIS | Set of HR modules linked together around a single personnel database |
| Payroll software | Tool that calculates payslips and produces social declarations |
| Time management software | Tool focused on working hours, schedules and time tracking |

Some vendors cover several of these families. Others focus on one and connect to neighbouring tools.

## The features of HR management software and the obligations they cover

Each module addresses a task of the HR department, and often an employer obligation. The rules quoted below come from official Service-Public guides.

| Feature | What it is for | Corresponding rule |
|---|---|---|
| Personnel file | Centralising each employee's information | The single staff register is mandatory from the first employee |
| Hiring | Preparing arrival documents | The pre-hiring declaration is sent at the earliest 8 days before hiring |
| Leave and absences | Tracking entitlements and requests | An employee earns 2.5 working days of leave per month of actual work |
| Working time | Tracking hours and overtime | Legal working time is 35 hours per week for a full-time job |
| Reviews | Scheduling and recording reviews | The career path review takes place every 4 years |
| Payroll | Preparing or calculating payslips | The payslip can be issued electronically, unless the employee objects |
| Social declarations | Sending data to the relevant bodies | The DSN is due on the 5th or the 15th of the following month, depending on headcount |
| Benefits | Managing meal vouchers, for example | The employer's share is between 50% and 60% of the voucher's value |

### What the software changes day to day

- Information is entered only once, then reused by the various modules.
- Deadlines are tracked automatically, such as the date of the next [career path review]({{< relref "career-path-review-france.md" >}}).
- Payroll data feeds directly into the [DSN (nominative social declaration)]({{< relref "dsn-definition-french-payroll-declaration.md" >}}).
- Arrival documents rely on the same data as the [hiring formalities]({{< relref "hiring-formalities-employee-france.md" >}}).

| Kelio | 20,000 clients | Not found |
| Factorial | More than 17,000 companies | Not found |
| Eurécia | More than 4,500 client companies and 400,000 users | 20 years of HR expertise |
| Lucca | More than 1,000,000 users | More than 20 years of expertise |

These figures were taken on 7 October 2026. They call for three precautions:

1. They are commercial statements, not verified by any third party.
2. They do not measure the same thing: some count companies, others users.
3. Some vendors operate in several countries, and their figures do not necessarily relate to France alone.

This table therefore does not show which software is the most used. It only indicates that each of these five vendors claims several thousand client companies.

### Which software for which situation?

- **Main need is payroll**: PayFit and Kelio include payroll in their offer.
- **Need to track time and schedules**: Kelio, Eurécia and Factorial publish dedicated modules.
- **Need to know the price in advance**: Lucca and Kelio publish the most detailed grids.
- **Need to test before deciding**: Eurécia, Factorial and PayFit offer a free trial.

## How to choose HR management software

### Start from processes, not features

1. List the tasks that take the most time: payroll, leave, schedules, reviews.
2. Identify the obligations that cause problems today: missed deadlines, documents that cannot be found.
3. Count the users concerned: employees, managers, administrators.
4. Identify the tools already in place with which the software will have to exchange data.

### Check data protection

HR management software processes personal data. The CNIL requires that only authorised people have access and that their actions are logged. These points are detailed in the article on [HR software and personal data]({{< relref "hr-software-personal-data-gdpr.md" >}}).

### Look at artificial intelligence features

Several vendors highlight AI features. Those involved in sorting applications are classified as high-risk by the European regulation, as explained in the article on [AI in recruitment]({{< relref "ai-recruitment-eu-ai-act.md" >}}).

### Ask for a comparable quote

Public prices are starting points. To compare two offers, the quote must cover the same number of employees and the same modules, and state the cost of deployment.

## Common mistakes

1. Choosing a very comprehensive tool when the real need is limited to payroll or leave.
2. Confusing software that prepares payroll with software that calculates it.
3. Relying on the number of clients announced by the vendor to judge quality.
4. Forgetting to check that the [French payslip]({{< relref "french-payslip-mandatory-information.md" >}}) and the DSN come out correctly from the new tool.
