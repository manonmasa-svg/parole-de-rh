> **In short:**
> 1. **PayFit** is a French payroll software and service that claims 22,000 customer companies. Its plans range from new businesses to companies with more than 1,000 employees (checked on 29 October 2026).
> 2. For a very small business, published prices range from €20 to €30 excluding VAT per employee per month, plus a €49 subscription, with no commitment. The Starter plan, for a first payroll, costs €26 per employee with no subscription.
> 3. Its experts produce payroll and social security returns, and the vendor says it takes responsibility for them.
> 4. Data is hosted in France and PayFit is ISO 27001 certified. Some collective agreements (construction, agriculture) and French overseas departments are not yet covered.

This **PayFit review** relies only on the vendor's public pages, consulted on 29 October 2026: home page, plans and pricing, security, and payroll service for mid-sized companies. The software was not tested and Parole de RH has no commercial relationship with PayFit. Customer testimonials published on the vendor's website are not reproduced.

## PayFit in a few declared figures

PayFit presents itself as a payroll and HR service: a team of experts runs payroll, an artificial intelligence assistant supports the company, and a platform centralises employee management.

| Figure declared by PayFit | Value checked on 29 October 2026 |
|---|---|
| Customer companies | 22,000 |
| Payroll experts | Over 200 |
| Regulatory updates per year | "+500" (plans page) or 600 (home page) |
| Integrations with other tools | Over 50, including Alan, Qonto, Pennylane and Swile |
| Announced migration time | 10 days (plans page) or 15 days (mid-sized company page) |
| Average live chat response time | 1 min 30 |

These figures are declared by the vendor. Two of them vary depending on the page consulted: the number of regulatory updates and the migration time. The figure of 22,000 companies is the same as the one checked on 7 and 12 October 2026 for the site's comparisons.

## PayFit plans and prices

PayFit now publishes its prices on its plans page, which was not the case on the pages consulted on 12 October 2026. The page's simulator is set to 3 employees by default; an employee means anyone who receives a payslip, including the director, interns and apprentices.

| Plan | Price per employee per month (excl. VAT) | Monthly subscription (excl. VAT) | Total for 3 employees (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter (first payroll) | €26 | None | €78 |
| Payroll | €20 | €49 | €109 |
| Advanced payroll | €27 | €49 | €130 |
| HR+ | €30 | €49 | €139 |

All these plans are presented with no commitment and free set-up. The monthly subscription covers automated social security returns, legal and collective-agreement monitoring, and handling of employee arrivals and departures.

The page's FAQ states that prices "change with your payroll" and that only exceptional adjustments may lead to extra costs. From 10 employees, PayFit invites companies to request a demo: prices for SMEs and mid-sized companies are therefore not shown.

### What each plan includes

- **Starter**: payroll, social security returns (DSN, pre-hiring declaration), leave and absences, contracts and HR documents, hiring simulation. The vendor reserves it for companies running their first payroll.
- **Payroll**: set-up by an expert, payroll produced by PayFit's experts, access to Payfit AI, online payslips, social security returns, reports and accounting entries.
- **Advanced payroll**: in addition, advanced absence management, time and activity tracking, HR document generation, electronic signature and API access.
- **HR+**: in addition, reviews, objectives, one-to-one meetings, 360-degree feedback and employee surveys.

Three options can be added: expenses at €3 per employee per month, premium services from €59 per month, and Payfit IT for employees' IT equipment.

## Payroll run by PayFit's experts

This is the main difference from the HRIS covered on this site. Lucca and Factorial prepare payroll without calculating it, as explained in the [Lucca review]({{< relref "lucca-review.md" >}}) and the [Factorial review]({{< relref "factorial-review.md" >}}). PayFit, by contrast, says it produces payroll itself.

On its home page, the vendor describes payroll "fully carried out by our experts": collecting variables, generating payslips, handling formalities with the authorities and guaranteed compliance. The company approves the amounts before paying salaries.

PayFit states that it takes responsibility for payrolls and returns, and its page for mid-sized companies adds "Urssaf penalties included". The exact terms of this guarantee are set out in the vendor's general terms, which were not consulted for this review.

The returns handled include the monthly and event-based DSN, explained in the article on the [DSN in France]({{< relref "dsn-definition-french-payroll-declaration.md" >}}), as well as the pre-hiring declaration. The information a payslip must contain is listed in the article on the [French payslip]({{< relref "french-payslip-mandatory-information.md" >}}).

## Limits stated by PayFit

The FAQ on the plans page itself mentions two coverage limits:

- **Collective agreements**: some very specific agreements are not yet integrated, including construction, agriculture, landscaping and real estate sales agents.
- **Overseas departments**: PayFit focuses on mainland France and does not yet cover French overseas departments.

A company in these sectors or based overseas should therefore check this point first. For these situations, the comparison of the [best payroll software]({{< relref "best-payroll-software-france.md" >}}) presents other vendors, including Silae, which announces 900 collective agreements.

## Security and data hosting

| Point checked | What PayFit states (security page, 29 October 2026) |
|---|---|
| Certification | ISO 27001 |
| Hosting | All data, including backups, in France |
| Hosting provider | Amazon Web Services, ISO 27001 certified |
| Continuity | Real-time replication across 3 separate data centres in France |
| Document backups | Every hour |
| Encryption | Data encrypted in transit and at rest |
| Internal access | Limited to authorised employees, via VPN with two-factor authentication |

Payroll software processes the personal and salary data of every employee. The applicable rules are explained in the article on [HR software and personal data]({{< relref "hr-software-personal-data-gdpr.md" >}}).

## Payfit AI, the built-in assistant

Access to Payfit AI is part of the Payroll plan, and therefore of the Advanced payroll and HR+ plans built on it. The Starter plan mentions an AI assistant available 24 hours a day. According to the home page, Payfit AI:

- spots payroll anomalies;
- carries out some tasks for the user, such as sending variable pay items or creating HR reports;
- answers questions from the company and its employees;
- makes recommendations under the pay transparency directive.

This last point refers to an EU text whose transposition is followed in the article on [pay transparency in France]({{< relref "pay-transparency-eu-directive-france.md" >}}). The vendor announces an assistant available 24 hours a day and a team of experts reachable Monday to Saturday.

## PayFit, Lucca or Factorial: three different approaches

| Criterion | PayFit | Lucca | Factorial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role in payroll | Produces payroll | Prepares payroll | Prepares payroll |
| Published prices | Up to 9 employees | A price per module, above 100 employees | Entry price only |
| Commitment | No commitment | No minimum term | Cancellation at any time |
| Announced hosting | France | France and Germany | European Union |
| Certification | ISO 27001 | ISO 27001 | ISO 27001, SOC 2, SOC 3 |

For a company that does not want to produce its own payroll, PayFit meets that need directly. For a company that keeps its payroll software or accounting firm and is looking for an HRIS, Lucca and Factorial sit upstream of payroll.
