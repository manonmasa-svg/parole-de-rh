## Best time clock software: how this ranking is built

This comparison is not based on testing the software. Parole de RH has not used any of these tools and has no commercial relationship with any vendor. The ranking relies on what each vendor publishes, using four verifiable criteria:

1. **Published time-tracking price**: price of the clock or time module, with a clearly stated billing unit.
2. **Clock-in options announced**: computer, mobile, tablet, physical clock, geolocation.
3. **Trial and commitment**: possibility to test, stated minimum term.
4. **Hosting and security stated**: hosting location and certifications.

| Rank | Software | Main reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Combo | Only vendor to publish the price of its time clock, per-site plans shown |
| 2 | Lucca | Published price for timesheets, no minimum term, virtual or physical clock |
| 3 | Kelio | Detailed grid by plan and size, hosting in France, but 3-year initial commitment |
| 4 | Eurécia | Entry price and 30-day trial, time tracking built into a broad HRIS |
| 5 | Skello | Entry prices shown and seasonal pause, but price unit not stated and trial converted automatically |
| 6 | Factorial | Full range of clock-in options, but two different entry prices on the same page |

The ideal **time clock software** depends mostly on the company's organisation: number of sites, field or office staff, need for a wall-mounted clock.

## Combo: the time clock price published

Combo targets restaurants, hotels and shops, especially multi-site ones. It is the only vendor in the comparison to publish the price of the time clock itself.

| Plan or option | Price checked on 3 November 2026 | What is announced |
|---|---|---|
| Time | From €68 per site per month | Scheduling, hours tracking, payroll export, messaging |
| People | From €89 per site per month | Time, plus electronic signature, working-time modulation, dashboards |
| Time clock (option) | €2 per employee per month | Clock-in and clock-out on tablet or smartphone |
| Combo Pay payroll | €19 per employee per month | Built-in payroll software, DSN |

- **Clock-in**: in 2 clicks on a tablet or smartphone, with digital signature, random photo and geolocation for mobile teams.
- **Count**: Combo bills "active" employees, i.e. those appearing at least once in the month on a schedule, using a smoothed monthly average.
- **Limit**: the hosting location is not stated on the page consulted, which only mentions "secure hosting".

Photos and geolocation at clock-in involve personal data. The rules to follow are explained in the article on [HR software and personal data]({{< relref "hr-software-personal-data-gdpr.md" >}}).

## Lucca: a timesheet module with no commitment

Lucca offers time tracking as a module of its HR suite, at €3.00 excluding VAT per employee per month for a company with more than 100 employees. The subscription is announced with no minimum term.

- **Entry modes**: self-declared, virtual clock or physical clock, depending on the employee's profile.
- **Pre-filled timesheets** with usual or scheduled hours, the employee only entering differences.
- **Counting** of overtime, night hours and public holidays worked, with alerts when there are discrepancies.

Details of the other modules and hosting are in the [Lucca review]({{< relref "lucca-review.md" >}}).

## Eurécia, Skello and Factorial: detailed analysis

### Eurécia: time tracking within a broad HRIS

Eurécia offers a Business plan from €4.20 excluding VAT per employee per month, for 11 to 500 employees. Its simulator shows an average price excluding VAT per user, sold in packs of 10. The "Time and activities" module is added to the HR portal.

Its time clock page announces online, one-click clocking in on the app, automatic timesheet filling, detection of missed clock-ins, a configurable lateness tolerance and overtime calculation according to company rules.

The 30-day free trial is presented as commitment-free. The FAQ, however, states that the subscription's commitment terms are set out in the contract.

### Skello: clock-in plans from €59 per month

Skello publishes three families of plans, with a 10% discount for an annual commitment:

| Plan | Price shown | Content announced |
|---|---|---|
| Badgeuse Standard | From €59 per month | Arrivals and departures on tablet and mobile, geolocation |
| Badgeuse Max | From €89 per month | Absence management, payroll variables, timesheet signature |
| Duo Standard (scheduling and clock-in) | From €99 per month | Compliant scheduling and clock-in |
| Duo Max | From €129 per month | Staffing forecasts, automatic scheduling |

- **Price unit**: the page does not state whether these amounts are per site or per company.
- **Trial**: 14 days free. According to the FAQ, the trial automatically converts into a subscription unless cancelled at the end of the period.
- **Seasonality**: the subscription can be paused for up to 9 months over a rolling 12 months, for customers of at least 3 months.

### Factorial: an entry price to check

Factorial announces clocking in and out from a computer, the mobile app or a QR code on a tablet, with a geolocation option.

On 3 November 2026, the text of its pricing page showed "from €2.9 per month per user", while the title of the same page said "from €6.5". On 25 October 2026, the text showed €6.9. The applicable price is therefore available on quote. The rest of the offer is detailed in the [Factorial review]({{< relref "factorial-review.md" >}}).

## What French law says about working time

Time clock software helps employers comply with working time rules. According to the Service-Public guide on working time for full-time employees, checked on 25 September 2025:

| Rule | General limit |
|---|---|
| Maximum daily working time | 10 hours a day, except for exemptions |
| Maximum weekly working time in a single week | 48 hours |
| Average weekly working time | 44 hours over 12 consecutive weeks |
| Collective working hours | Posting of start and end times and rest periods |

In a dispute over unpaid overtime, the employee may provide any evidence, including time clock records, according to the Service-Public guide on overtime. Calculation and premiums are covered in the article on [overtime pay in France]({{< relref "overtime-pay-france-rules.md" >}}).

## Time clock software: criteria for choosing well

1. **Billing method**: per site (Combo), per employee (Kelio, Lucca, Eurécia) or per month with no unit stated (Skello). The total cost depends heavily on the company's organisation.
2. **Clock-in method**: wall-mounted clock, shared tablet, smartphone or self-declared entry. Field staff need a mobile solution.
3. **Commitment**: from no minimum term (Lucca) to 3 years (Kelio). Trial terms matter too: at Skello, the trial becomes a subscription unless cancelled.
4. **Personal data**: geolocation and photos at clock-in involve employees' personal data.
5. **Link with payroll**: export of hours and variables to the payroll software or accounting firm.
