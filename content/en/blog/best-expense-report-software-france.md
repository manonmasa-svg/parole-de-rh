## Best expense report software: how this ranking is built

Naming the **best expense report software** means stating the criteria. Comparison site ratings and testimonials published by vendors are not used. The ranking uses four criteria that anyone can check on the vendor's website:

1. **Price transparency**: published price, billing unit, company size covered.
2. **Trial and commitment**: free trial, commitment period, monthly or annual billing.
3. **Published features**: automatic receipt reading, mileage, approval workflow, archiving, exports to payroll and accounting.
4. **Stated security**: certification, data hosting location.

A vendor that publishes a full price list ranks ahead of one that refers to a quote. This rank measures the amount of verifiable information published, not the quality of the software in use.

## Rydoo, Pleo and Lucca: public price lists

### Rydoo: from €8 per user, from 5 users

Rydoo publishes two self-service plans, from 5 users: Essentials at €8 per user per month billed annually (€10 monthly) and Pro at €10 (€12 monthly). The page does not say whether these prices exclude VAT.

Essentials includes receipt scanning, unlimited expenses, mileage allowances and a single approval workflow. Pro adds per diems, multi-level approvals and automatic transaction matching. The Business (from 30 active users) and Enterprise (from 50) plans are tailored, with billing limited to active users.

### Pleo: a payment card and three plans

Pleo combines physical and virtual payment cards with expense management. Its three plans, with a free trial, are shown per user per month:

| Plan | Price | Billing | Points found |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | €8 | Monthly only, 1 administrator maximum | Cards, automated expense reports, accounting integrations |
| Build | €14 | Annual, 3 users minimum | Adds reimbursements, mileage and approval workflows |
| Optimise | €18 | Annual, 3 users minimum | Adds budgets and HRIS integrations |

Reimbursing expenses paid by employees only appears from the Build plan. Pleo reports more than 40,000 client companies in Europe. Its card-issuing company is an electronic money institution authorised in Denmark.

### Lucca: a module of its HRIS

Lucca charges its business expenses module €5.81 excl. VAT per claimant per month, in its indicative price list for companies with more than 100 employees. The payment methods module works with expense reports and is among the services included at no extra cost. The subscription is announced with no minimum term. Other details of the offer are covered in the [Lucca review]({{< relref "lucca-review.md" >}}).

## PayFit and Eurécia: expense reports in a payroll or HR tool

### PayFit: an option of its payroll

PayFit offers expense reports as an option, at €3 per employee per month. According to its offers page, the module is billed on the total number of employees in the establishment and linked to payroll: the impact on payslips is visible in real time. The page also mentions a currency converter, mid-month reimbursement and a spending limit policy. PayFit's plans are announced with no commitment. The module is reserved for companies using PayFit payroll, presented in the [PayFit review]({{< relref "payfit-review.md" >}}).

### Eurécia: the expense reports module of its HRIS

Eurécia includes expense reports in its HRIS. Its dedicated page announces automatic receipt reading, mileage scales based on the Urssaf scale or customised, caps by type of expense, automatic meal voucher counts and exports to the main accounting software. The price of the module alone is not shown outside the simulator: the Business plan starts at €4.20 excl. VAT per employee per month, all modules combined. Details are in the [Eurécia review]({{< relref "eurecia-review.md" >}}).

## What Urssaf says about business expenses

Expense report software mainly helps keep receipts, which determine the exemption from contributions. According to the Urssaf page on business expenses, updated on 30 October 2026, the employer can reimburse them in two ways:

- **At actual cost, with receipts**: the reimbursement is excluded from contributions if the employer proves the employee had to incur these expenses and produces the receipts. There is no exemption cap.
- **As a flat-rate allowance**: the exemption applies within limits set each year.

| Situation (2026 amounts) | Exemption limit |
|---|---|
| Meal taken at the workplace due to constraints | €7.50 per meal |
| Employee on a business trip who has to eat in a restaurant | €21.40 per meal |
| Employee on a business trip who does not have to eat in a restaurant | €10.40 per meal |
| Remote work, allowance not provided for by a collective agreement | €2.70 per day, up to €59.40 per month |

These amounts change every year. The rules on [remote work in France]({{< relref "remote-work-france-employer-refusal.md" >}}) and [meal vouchers]({{< relref "meal-vouchers-rules-france.md" >}}) are covered in dedicated articles.
