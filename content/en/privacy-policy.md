## Data controller

The data controller is the publisher of the site, Parole de RH, who can be contacted at [contact@parole-de-rh.fr](mailto:contact@parole-de-rh.fr).

## What the site does not collect

Parole de RH offers no account creation, no form and no newsletter. The site uses no audience measurement tool, no social media button and no embedded third-party content (video, map, advertising). It sets no cookies: details are given on the [Cookies]({{< relref "cookies.md" >}}) page.

The internal search engine runs entirely in the browser: the words typed are sent neither to the publisher nor to any third-party service.

## Data processed

### Messages sent to the contact address

When someone writes to the contact address, the publisher receives their email address, their name if it appears and the content of the message.

- **Purpose**: replying to the message.
- **Recipient**: the publisher only. Messages are not sold, transferred or used for commercial purposes.
- **Hosting**: the mailbox is hosted by OVHcloud, which sets out its commitments in its [data protection policy](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/personal-data-protection/).
- **Retention**: messages are kept for as long as needed to reply and follow up on the exchange.

### Technical connection data

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any host, GitHub may record technical connection data, including the IP address, in particular for security purposes. This processing is GitHub's responsibility and is described in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The publisher has no access to this data.

Fonts and images are hosted on the site itself: viewing a page does not connect to any other service.

## Links to other sites

Articles link to official sites (Service-Public, Légifrance, CNIL, Urssaf) and to the websites of the vendors mentioned. These sites apply their own privacy policy, over which the publisher of Parole de RH has no control.

## Your rights

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives everyone rights over their data, which the CNIL, the French data protection authority, presents on its page about [rights to control your personal data](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/les-droits-pour-maitriser-vos-donnees-personnelles):

- the right of access;
- the right to rectification;
- the right to erasure;
- the right to object;
- the right to restriction;
- the right to data portability.

To exercise them, simply write to [contact@parole-de-rh.fr](mailto:contact@parole-de-rh.fr). In case of difficulty, a complaint can be lodged with the CNIL, following the procedure described on its page [Adresser une plainte à la CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/adresser-une-plainte).

## Changes to this policy

This policy may change, in particular if the site adds a service that processes data. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
