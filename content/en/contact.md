To report an error, suggest a topic or ask a question about an article, write to:

[contact@parole-de-rh.fr](mailto:contact@parole-de-rh.fr)

Parole de RH does not provide personalised legal advice. For an individual situation, readers should contact the labour inspectorate or a legal professional.

Software vendors, training providers and HR businesses interested in a partnership can [write to the same address](mailto:contact@parole-de-rh.fr?subject=Partnership). Any content published in that context is clearly labelled as such, in line with the [editorial policy]({{< relref "editorial-policy.md" >}}).
