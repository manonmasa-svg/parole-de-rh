## How this questionnaire works

- The software suggested is the software covered in the Parole de RH comparisons: payroll software, HRIS, HR software and recruitment software.
- The need you choose selects the software covered in the matching comparison.
- Company size and your main criterion separate that software. A product moves up when the vendor publishes a plan for that company size or shows the information you are looking for (price, trial, hosting, service) on its public pages.
- In case of a tie, the order of the comparison applies.
- Each result shows the date on which the information was checked.

## What this questionnaire does not do

- It does not collect any data. Your answers stay in your browser: they are neither sent nor stored.
- It does not replace a demo or a quote. Vendors' prices and terms may change after the date they were checked.
- Information marked "not found" in a comparison means it was not on the pages consulted, not that it does not exist.
