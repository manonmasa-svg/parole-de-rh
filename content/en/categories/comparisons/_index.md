## How these comparisons are made

Each Parole de RH comparison ranks solutions on verifiable criteria, rather than on an impression from use.

- **Verifiable criteria**: published price, free trial and minimum term, hosting and security stated, features announced, target companies.
- **Dated checks**: information comes from vendors' public pages, and each article gives the date it was checked. "Not found" means the information did not appear on the pages consulted.
- **No testing, no commercial relationship**: the software and cards compared were not tested, and Parole de RH has no commercial relationship with any vendor or issuer.
- **Official sources for HR schemes**: comparisons of contracts, terminations or employee benefits rely on the official Service-Public guides of the French government.
