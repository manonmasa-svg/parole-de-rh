## An independent website about human resources

Parole de RH is an independent news website dedicated to human resources in France. It is not affiliated with any software vendor, consulting firm or training provider.

The site is written for HR managers, heads of small and medium-sized businesses, managers and employees who want to understand the rules that apply to them.

## What the site publishes

- Explainers on French HR news: recruitment, training, payroll, employment law, onboarding, management, workplace wellbeing, compensation.
- Content about HR software and HRIS.
- Comparisons between schemes, contracts or tools.
- Definitions that explain French HR acronyms and concepts.

## How articles are written

Every article relies on official sources that can be consulted online (Service-Public, Légifrance, CNIL, Urssaf, European institutions). Sources are cited and linked at the end of each article, with the date they were consulted. The method is detailed on the [Editorial policy]({{< relref "editorial-policy.md" >}}) page.

## Who writes the articles

Articles are published under pen names. Each byline corresponds to an editorial area of the site, not to an identified individual. No degree, professional experience or career history is attributed to them.
