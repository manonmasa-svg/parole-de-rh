## How to read this calendar

- Each deadline links to the official guide it was taken from.
- Past dates disappear from the page automatically.
- When a deadline depends on the DSN filing date, only the month is shown: the exact day depends on the company's headcount.
- A collective agreement or a company agreement may set different dates. This calendar presents the general rules and does not replace personalised advice.
